《两次一人扛》 - Comparison View

Professional hand-drawn sketchnote comparison on pure white. Black ink line work
with slight wobble, à la Mike Rohde sketchnoting. Diagram-style visuals ONLY —
no realistic or photographic images.

PALETTE: mono-ink — black ink dominant, sparse semantic accents
COLORS: Pure White background (#FFFFFF), Near Black (#1A1A1A) for all outlines,
        text, figures, arrows, and doodles; Coral Red (#E8655A) reserved for
        loss/failure markers only; Muted Teal (#5FA8A8) reserved for win markers
        only. Color accents MUST stay under 10% of the canvas. Except for these
        two accent colors, use no other color anywhere.

LAYOUT: Vertical split down the middle with a slightly wobbly hand-drawn divider
line. Left column headed by oversized hand-lettered "2024 巴黎"; right column
headed by oversized hand-lettered "2026 名古屋".

LEFT SIDE (2024):
- One stick figure standing alone, role label above: "樊振东"
- Small tag beneath: "男单只剩他一个中国人"
- Hand-lettered scale depicting what he carried: "一场球的输赢"
- Simple chain of three small boxes in teal outline showing his path:
  "1/4 决赛 4:3" , "半决赛 4:0" , "决赛 4:1"
- Large handwritten result marker at bottom-left in teal: "守住"

RIGHT SIDE (2026):
- One stick figure standing alone, role label above: "王楚钦"
- Small tag beneath: "要求他一个人拿两分"
- Hand-lettered scale depicting what he carried: "一整支队伍的输赢"
- Simple chain of three small boxes showing his two matches, two of them in
  coral red outline: "第1盘 3:2" , "第4盘 0:3"
- Same stick figure drawn smaller with a dashed outline beside it labelled
  "其余三人" in plain black
- Large handwritten result marker at bottom-right in coral red: "没接住"

BOTTOM: One single-line hand-lettered tagline centered under both columns:
"同样只有一个人，重量从没被分摊过"

STYLE: Hand-lettered headings (bold, oversized), handwritten body annotations,
        generous white space, no computer fonts, no gradients, no shadows,
        no 3D effects. Clean composition with generous margins and breathing room.

TEXT SAFETY (critical):

  "要求他一个人拿两分", "一场球的输赢", "一整支队伍的输赢", "1/4 决赛 4:3",
